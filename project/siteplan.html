Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Liquid Gold Filling Station Management System

I chose this name because it represents a modern filling station that provides quality fuel products, excellent customer service, cashier services, and an efficient business management system.

Optional Domain: liquidgoldstation.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about Liquid Gold Filling Station, including fuel products, fuel prices, customer services, cashier responsibilities, safety information, contact details, and the Business Management System used to manage fuel sales, inventory, reports, and daily station operations.

3. Scenarios

  • What fuel products and services are available at Liquid Gold Filling Station?
  • What services does the account clerk provide for customers?
  • What information can the Business Management System manage?
  • Where is the filling station located and what are the operating hours?
  • How can customers contact the filling station?

4. Color Schema

  • Yellow (#FFD700) – Logo, headings, highlights and important sections.
  • Red (#D62828) – Header, navigation, footer and buttons.
  • White (#FFFFFF) – Background color.
  • Dark Gray (#333333) – Paragraph text.

5. Typography

  • Poppins – Headings and Navigation
  • Roboto – Body text and paragraphs
