Site Name Liquid Gold Filling Station Management System Reason I chose this name because it represents a filling station that provides quality fuel products, excellent customer service, and efficient business management. The website will help customers learn about the station, its services, and how technology supports its daily operations. Site Purpose The website provides information about Liquid Gold Filling Station, including fuel products and services, fuel prices, operating hours, safety tips, contact information, business management operations, and the responsibilities of the account clerk. It will also explain how the Business Management System supports daily operations, inventory management, fuel sales, and customer service. Scenarios 1. What fuel products and services are available at Liquid Gold Filling Station? 2. What are the operating hours and where is Liquid Gold Filling Station located? 3. What services does the account clerk provide for customers at Liquid Gold Filling Station? 4. What information and reports can the Business Management System provide to improve the station's daily operations? 5. How can customers contact the station or make inquiries about fuel prices and services?
